The controls that keep a company safe don't have to live in meetings. Here is a ledger of six systems built with them in files instead, and the unfinished ones booked as plans.

One is a CMS where AI agents are users with permissions, so their edits arrive as diffs a person accepts or rejects. Another is a legal research tool that can pin every prompt to a local model. A third is a CSV editor that is finished and archived.

What they share is where the controls live: rules in files, automation checking every change, agents opening pull requests, and a named person approving anything that leaves the building.

One idea to borrow: pick the recurring meeting whose real job is catching a mistake, write that mistake down as a check a script can run, and keep the meeting until the check has caught something twice.

The ledger, screenshots included, is worth a read.
