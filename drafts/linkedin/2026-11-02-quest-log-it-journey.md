Our founder's open-source curriculum publishes its own failing grade: 0 of 27 tested slices perfect, after artificial intelligence (AI) agents took the course.

IT-Journey is Amr Abdel-Motaleb's free course of hands-on information technology (IT) quests, built in the open since 2020. The agents take the course in a sandbox, run the commands and report what breaks. A fix is kept only when a scripted score holds or rises, never because the model graded its own work. Every agent lane stays off until a person sets its switch.

The testing loop has been idle since September, and the grade stays on the page. A separate daily AI review, run on October 29, rated the repository 72 out of 100. Two bugs it found were fixed the next day, and six issues wait on a person.

If you are weighing AI for work that has to be right, the write-up shows how the checks and the switches work.
