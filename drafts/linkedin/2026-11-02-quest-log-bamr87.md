Our founder, Amr Abdel-Motaleb, runs 44 software projects from one system, and it puts its own problems on the front page. On November 1: 20 projects needing attention, 6 automated jobs failing.

That is the useful part. It shows what governed artificial intelligence (AI) looks like day to day. The rules are written down, and they change only when a person approves the change. Every scheduled AI task has an off switch and a spending cap. The AI proposes changes for review, and anything that touches a budget, a credential or a setting waits for a person.

When a daily AI review examined the system itself, it found the rule checks passing while the records they guard had drifted. It filed what it found for a person to decide.

The write-up shows each of those guardrails on screen, along with every finding the review filed against the system.
